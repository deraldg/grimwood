A family portal and living archive

Home base for family, memory, making, and study.

Grimwood Family is a friendly entry point for Derald and Kathy's family history, shared photos, personal projects, faith and reflection, creative work, Oregon places, and the technical systems that grew alongside the family story.

Open by design

The site starts with Derald and Kathy's family center, then opens outward so children, grandchildren, and other family members can have their own spaces without forcing everything into one rigid tree.
