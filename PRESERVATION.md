# Preservation Notes

The current hosted `grimwood.family` root contains old public files and several
project/family folders. Cleanup should be staged, not destructive.

## Root Replacement Policy

These files may be replaced from this repository:

- `index.html`
- `default.html`
- `assets/site.css`

Do not overwrite `.htaccess` without reviewing host routing for `derald.com`.

## Preserve Or Review

- `dt`: DotTalk material.
- `x64base`: x64base material.
- `x64base-site`: older website material.
- `derald.com`: generated DotTalk++ document site staging.
- `grimwood_ged`: genealogy.
- `photos`, `photo_gallery`: family and place photos.
- `jenny`, `nathan`, `willow`: family-member spaces.

## Archive Later

After inspection, older template folders such as `about_me`, `contact`, `links`,
and `resume` can either be preserved under a dated archive or rewritten as pages
inside the new portal.
